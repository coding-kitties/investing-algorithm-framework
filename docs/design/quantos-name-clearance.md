# QuantOS preliminary name-clearance research

- Research date: 2026-11-28
- Requested markets: Benelux, European Union, United Kingdom, United States
- Status: **Incomplete screening; not legal clearance**

## Executive summary

This research does not establish that QuantOS is free to use, nor does it
establish infringement.

- A successful US federal search for `CM:quantos OR CM:"quant os"` returned
  six records, all displayed as dead. That is a favorable exact-name screening
  result, not a clearance conclusion.
- WIPO Madrid Monitor shows an **active international QUANTOS registration**,
  number **970694**, held by Mettler-Toledo GmbH in classes 9 and 37.
  International registration status does not establish protection in every
  designated territory; the individual designation histories remain unchecked.
- A separate, publicly advertised **paid QuantOS trading-analysis product**
  exists at [quantoslabs.com](https://quantoslabs.com/). Its own website identifies
  the developer as Yathis Corp. This is a direct same-name, related-field use,
  not merely an inactive GitHub repository.
- Existing QuantOS-branded open-source investment research and trading projects
  also remain discoverable.
- Similar-name US software/service marks require assessment. In particular,
  **QUANTON OS** is a live application for AI/business-analytics SaaS.
- Benelux, EU, and UK official-register name searches were not completed because
  of access failures. US state registers and a comprehensive unregistered-use
  search were not completed.

**Recommended next step:** obtain a focused professional assessment of the
same-field use at quantoslabs.com, complete the missing register searches, and
review similar software marks before treating the rename as cleared.

## Scope and evidence standard

The proposed name is QuantOS, replacing Investing Algorithm Framework, for an
open-source Python quantitative research, backtesting, and trading framework.
Potential hosted integrations are relevant to the search, but this report does
not assume that the framework itself provides regulated financial services.

Software classes 9 and 42 are initial screening areas. Class 36 is relevant
where actual financial services are offered. Shared class numbers alone do not
establish a conflict, and related goods/services can fall in different classes.
The successful US exact-name search did not restrict classes or live/dead status.

Evidence is distinguished as:

- **Official record observed:** register information displayed in an official
  website during this research.
- **First-party statement:** information published by the product/project itself;
  not independent verification of its claims, customers, legal rights, or launch
  history.
- **Third-party lead:** search-index information requiring official verification.
- **Unresolved:** a search or legal question that was not completed.

No trademark professional was engaged. No parties were contacted, accounts
created, paid searches purchased, or legal opinions obtained.

## Interpretation and unresolved risks

- **Same-field use:** quantoslabs.com warrants priority review because both
  offerings use QuantOS for trading/research software.
- **Registered and pending marks:** the active international QUANTOS record and
  similar software/service marks need territorial and goods/services analysis.
- **Unregistered rights:** the
  [USPTO's official clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks)
  explicitly includes common-law use in the United States. No equivalent
  cross-jurisdiction legal conclusion is assumed here.
- **No certainty from missing results:** an inaccessible register, incomplete
  web search, dead US record, small repository, or PyPI reservation does not
  establish clearance.
- **No priority determination:** renaming now does not establish priority over
  earlier users. This research does not determine any party's priority.

No numerical risk score, infringement finding, or permission to use the name is
provided. The evidence does not support describing QuantOS as completely clear.

## Work needed to complete a professional clearance

1. Define the actual distribution/promotion territories and exact software,
   hosted-service, and financial-service offerings.
2. Complete BOIP, EUIPO, EU national-office/TMview, and UK exact and similarity
   searches; retain dated exports with owners, status, and full specifications.
3. Inspect international registration 970694 designation histories and related
   national records, rather than assuming international status applies locally.
4. Expand US spelling, phonetic, visual, and related-goods searches; review the
   full records behind the similar-name candidates and any further relevant hits.
5. Check US state trademark/business registers, recent publication/application
   records, relevant company/trade-name registers, domains, and broader
   unregistered commercial use.
6. Establish the ownership, chronology, actual use, geographic reach, and any
   rights of quantoslabs.com and the other same-name projects.
7. Ask a qualified trademark professional for a written territory-specific
   assessment of the evidence. A clearance opinion can be commissioned without
   registering a trademark.
8. Recheck close to launch; new filings and uses can appear after this date.
